## 7. Pitfalls met

Two jobs of ours at once on the 20 GB GPU are fine, three thrash; parallel notebook runs must not share a scratch file; a Bash call with `sleep` over a few seconds is blocked (use an `until` loop in the background); `pkill -f` patterns match the shell that contains them (use `[x]` brackets or PIDs); `rm` with a variable is blocked by the safety check; notebooks print no progress while they run (the Stage_Evaluation of 332 photos took 72 minutes); the research loader needs `NUDT_DIR` for the model checkpoints even for official data; `SE_N_PHOTOS` must exceed the photo count so all photos are sampled; RANSAC is not deterministic (re-runs differ by a photo or two, once by a wrong answer at bar 4); the "official" word means the OLD set everywhere on the results page, the new set is "high-altitude"; Fable evaluated frozen thresholds once on the locked test split.
